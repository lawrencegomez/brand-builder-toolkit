# Resources

Share the relevant links at the moment they help, not all at once. When the person
finds something they like, ask them to paste the link back so you can analyze it.

## Website inspiration (Phase 2)
| Site | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/) | Browsing real website styles by look and feel; great for "I want it to feel like this" |
| [Godly](https://godly.website/) | Bold, modern, motion-heavy sites |
| [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/) | Award-winning, experimental work (good for scroll and 3D ideas) |
| [Land-book](https://land-book.com/) | Landing pages sorted by industry and style |

## Typography (Phase 4)
| Site | Use it for |
|---|---|

| [Open Foundry](https://open-foundry.com/) | Curated free open-source fonts with character |

## Rules for anything you download
- Fonts: confirm the license allows web use. Google Fonts and Open Foundry fonts are
  open-source; Fontshare fonts are free under the ITF Free Font License.
- Icons and images: record source and license in `assets/LICENSES.md`, and add any
  required credit to the site.
- Never copy another site's logo, photos, or text.
